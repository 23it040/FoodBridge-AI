## 📐 Pickup Request Form Modal in Figma

- **Modal Title**: `"Submit Food Pickup Request"`
- **Food Summary Bar**: Food Title, Quantity, Expiry clock, Donor establishment name.
- **Form Inputs**:
  - `Estimated Beneficiaries`: Number input (e.g. *60 people to feed*).
  - `Contact Person Name & Phone`: Direct contact numbers.
  - `Preferred Pickup Time Slot`: Time range picker.
  - `Transport Vehicle Type`: Dropdown (`Two-wheeler`, `Van`, `Refrigerated Truck`, `On Foot`).
  - `Notes for Donor`: Textarea for special instructions.
- **Submit CTA**: `"Submit Pickup Request"` (Solid Mint button).
