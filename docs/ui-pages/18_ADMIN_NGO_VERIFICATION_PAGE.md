## 📐 Page Structure & Modals in Figma

- **Header Section**: Page Title & Subtitle `"Review and verify legal documents submitted by NGO partners."`
- **Pending Review Table**:
  - NGO Organization Name & Registration Number.
  - Contact Person Name, Email & Phone.
  - Submitted Document Links (e.g., `Govt Registration.pdf`, `Tax Exemption.pdf`).
- **Actions & Modals**:
  - `Approve NGO` Button (1-click green approve button).
  - `Reject NGO Verification Modal`: Rejection Reason textarea (e.g. *"Expired registration doc"*), submit rejection trigger.
